How to read this

  • Covers visits tagged utm_source=chatgpt with utm_campaign=ltd_company (plus an early chatgpt_LTDads test) since 5 Oct. Mixpanel remembers the tags for the rest of the visit, so pages after the landing page are counted too.
  • Before / after the switch: the ad first linked to /company, which forwards to the formation page and drops the tags from the address. It now links straight to /features/limited-company-set-up.
  • Meetings booked only count bookings whose link carried the ChatGPT tags. The formation page's "Talk to our team" link doesn't pass them yet, so clicks on it are shown as the intent signal.
  • Sign-ups count new app accounts that came through a link carrying the tags (e.g. the BTL calculator page's Set up today). Only covers people who accepted cookies, and includes internal tests.
