How to read this

  • Only people who accept the cookie banner are tracked in Mixpanel, so visitors and clicks are lower than the real numbers. Meetings and plans come from HubSpot and are complete.
  • Source comes from the link the visitor arrived on (UTM tags — Mixpanel keeps them for the whole visit), otherwise the site they came from. Site banner = clicked the sitewide Black Friday banner up to 30 minutes before reaching the page; that wins over older tags.
  • Book a call counts the booking buttons (and the older “Talk to our team” buttons). Claim online counts the claim/checkout buttons.
  • Meetings are bookings made through the page’s booking button: the link carries utm_medium=black_friday_page or bf_cost_comparison, plus the visitor’s own source and campaign. Tagging started on 17 Nov at 12:47 (the page update); bookings made through the page before then can’t be told apart. Banner, search and direct visitors all arrive untagged, so their meetings show as “Website / untagged”.
  • Plans bought = a Closed won Plans-pipeline deal on the contact, created from an hour before the booking onwards.
  • Internal test visits and bookings count too.
